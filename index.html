How to use this page

      

        You need two things from GTO Wizard: your spot, and its
        JSON. This page turns that JSON into a PioSOLVER range. Four steps.
      


      
    
        
  1. 
          Open your spot in GTO Wizard and wait until you can see the
          solution on the screen.
        
    2. 
        
  2. 
          Open the Network tab - press F12 (on a Mac:
          ⌘+⌥+I), then click
          Network at the top of the panel that appeared. Nothing happened?
          Right-click the page → Inspect.
          
          
          
            New to this? See the official pages:
            how to open DevTools
            and the
            Network tab
            (Chrome, Edge, Brave and Arc work the same way).
          
        
    3. 
        
  3. 
          Type spot in the filter box. A request called
          spot-solution appears. Click it, open Response,
          select everything (Ctrl+A) and copy it
          (Ctrl+C).
        
    4. 
        
  4. 
          Paste it, press Convert, copy the range. Paste into the big box
          on the left (yes, it is long - that is normal), press Convert,
          tick the colours you want, then Copy and paste that into
          PioSOLVER. Done!
        
    5. 
      


      

        Nothing in the list? DevTools only records what happens while it is
        open: keep it open and press F5 to reload the page - then
        spot-solution shows up.
        

        No spot at hand? Press Load GTO Wizard sample and
        watch the whole thing work.
        

        Reading the grid: every colour is one action, and the height of a
        slice is how often that hand takes it.
