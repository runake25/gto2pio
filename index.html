How to use the converter

      

        Grab the JSON of the spot you want from GTO Wizard, paste it on the left, tick the
        actions that belong in the range, then copy the result into PioSOLVER. Everything
        happens in this browser tab - nothing is uploaded anywhere.
      


      
1 - Get the payload out of GTO Wizard

      
    
        
  1. 
          Open the spot you want in GTO Wizard and let the solution you
          want to convert load on screen.
        
    2. 
        
  2. 
          Open the browser developer tools and switch to the Network tab
          (F12, or Ctrl+Shift+I;
          Cmd+Option+I on macOS).
        
    3. 
        
  3. 
          Filter the request list by spot - type
          spot into the Network filter box. The request called
          spot-solution is the one that carries the solution.
          
            Nothing in the list? The Network tab only shows requests that happen while it is
            open: leave it open and reload the page (F5) or reopen the spot, and
            the spot-solution request appears.
          
        
    4. 
        
  4. 
          Open that request's response and copy the whole JSON.
          Click spot-solution, open the Response tab, then copy
          everything - right click → Copy, or click inside the response and press
          Ctrl+A then Ctrl+C.
          
            You want the JSON that contains action_solutions and
            players_info. The converter names the format it recognised right
            after converting, so you see immediately whether you copied the right thing.
          
        
    5. 
      


      
2 - Paste it and convert

      
    
        
  1. 
          Click into the Paste JSON box on the left and paste
          (Ctrl+V) - the whole response, however long it is. A real
          spot response is around 200 KB, which is normal.
        
    2. 
        
  2. 
          Press Convert (or Ctrl+Enter). The status
          line then reports what was detected: the format, the number of hands and the
          actions of that spot.
        
    3. 
        
  3. 
          Nothing at hand? Load GTO Wizard sample converts a real response
          so you can see the whole flow before pasting your own.
        
    4. 
      


      
3 - Pick the actions and copy the range

      
    
        
  1. 
          Every action of the spot is listed with its own colour, its own share of the range
          and its own range text. Tick the ones the range should contain - call plus the
          raises is what you get by default.
        
    2. 
        
  2. 
          Shortcuts: Raise + Call, Continues (everything
          except fold), All and None.
        
    3. 
        
  3. 
          Hit Copy or Download .txt and paste the range
          into PioSOLVER's range field.
        
    4. 
      


      
Where the numbers come from

      

        GTO Wizard reports a hand's action frequencies relative to the combos that hand
        still has at that node, and reports the combo totals separately. The converter uses
        both: a hand that is only half in range at this node also keeps half its weight,
        which is what PioSOLVER expects. The payload's own combo totals are the check - if a
        range cannot be reproduced from them, an amber warning appears next to
        Convert instead of a silently wrong range.
      


      
Reading the 13×13 grid

      

        Every cell is a hand and every coloured slice is one of the actions you ticked,
        with the slice height showing that hand's real split (a hand that folds 60% and
        calls 40% shows two slices in that proportion). The colours match the tick boxes,
        so the range you are about to copy is visible at a glance.
      


      
What the options do

      

        
Merge suited+offsuit on equal weight

        
AKs and AKo both at 50% become a single entry, AK:0.5.

        
Average per-combo data

        
Combo-level payloads (AcKd, AhKs, ...) are averaged into their hand class.

        
Decimals

        
Decimals kept on the weights: 2 gives AA:0.33, 0 gives plain AA.

        
Min weight

        
Drops hands below that frequency: 0.05 hides anything under 5%.

        
Scale

        
How the payload's numbers are read: auto guesses, fraction uses 0-1 as-is, percent divides by 100.

      


      
Good to know

      
    
        
  • A response covers the whole spot: the strategy arrays belong to the player who acts at that node, and that is the range you get.
    • 
        
  • The conversion runs entirely in your browser - your JSON is never uploaded and never sent to a server.
    • 
        
  • One node at a time: for another street or the other player, grab that spot's payload the same way.
    • 
        
  • The Inspector under the range shows what was detected (format, score, the hand order it used, notes and warnings) whenever a payload looks off.
    • 
        
  • It converts JSON to a Pio range, not the other way round.
