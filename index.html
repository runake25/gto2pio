How to use the converter

      

        Three steps: get the JSON out of GTO Wizard, paste it here, tick the actions you
        want in the range. Everything happens in this browser tab.
      


      
    
        
  1. 
          Copy the solution JSON out of GTO Wizard.
          Open the node you care about, then grab the JSON of the solution response
          (DevTools → Network → the request that returns
          action_solutions). It is the payload with the strategy
          arrays and the players_info block.
        
    2. 
        
  2. 
          Paste it into the box and press Convert
          (or Ctrl+Enter). The layout is detected on its own and the
          tool reports how many hands it read.
        
    3. 
        
  3. 
          Tick the actions that belong in the range.
          Each one has its own colour, its own share and its own range text, then hit
          Copy or Download .txt and paste the result into
          PioSOLVER's range field.
        
    4. 
      


      
Where the numbers come from

      

        GTO Wizard reports a hand's action frequencies relative to the combos that hand
        still has at that node, and reports the combo totals separately. The converter uses
        both: a hand that is only half in range at this node also keeps half its weight,
        which is what PioSOLVER expects. The payload's own combo totals are the check - if a
        range cannot be reproduced from them, an amber warning appears next to
        Convert instead of a silently wrong range.
      


      
Reading the 13×13 grid

      

        Every cell is a hand and every coloured slice is one of the actions you ticked,
        with the slice height showing that hand's real split (a hand that folds 60% and
        calls 40% shows two slices in that proportion). The colours match the tick boxes,
        so the range you are about to copy is visible at a glance.
      


      
What the options do

      

        
Merge suited+offsuit on equal weight

        
AKs and AKo both at 50% become a single entry, AK:0.5.

        
Average per-combo data

        
Combo-level payloads (AcKd, AhKs, ...) are averaged into their hand class.

        
Decimals

        
Decimals kept on the weights: 2 gives AA:0.33, 0 gives plain AA.

        
Min weight

        
Drops hands below that frequency: 0.05 hides anything under 5%.

        
Scale

        
How the payload's numbers are read: auto guesses, fraction uses 0-1 as-is, percent divides by 100.

      


      
Good to know

      
    
        
  • The conversion runs entirely in your browser - your JSON is never uploaded.
    • 
        
  • One node at a time: pick the street and the player in GTO Wizard before copying.
    • 
        
  • The Inspector under the range shows what was detected (format, score, the hand order it used, notes and warnings) whenever a payload looks off.
    • 
        
  • It converts JSON to a Pio range, not the other way round.
